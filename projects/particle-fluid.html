Free-surface flow, particle by particle.

An SPH extension to a C++ CFD codebase for simulating fluid motion around changing boundaries.

The idea

Particle methods are useful when a fluid boundary is moving or no longer neatly described by a fixed grid. This project explored that approach for free-surface flow and fluid transport.

The implementation

I implemented a Smooth Particle Hydrodynamics algorithm in an existing C++ computational fluid dynamics codebase, then worked with a team of three to validate the accuracy and performance of the result.

  • Extended the solver with particle-based free-surface behavior.
  • Worked through domain, cell, pressure, and boundary representations.
  • Validated the implementation against simulated flow behavior.
